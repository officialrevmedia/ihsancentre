Sponsor the Centre

Questions about giving

Is Al-Ihsan Learning Centre a registered charity?

Yes. We are a registered Canadian charity, registration number 763444353RR0001. You can confirm this on the Canada Revenue Agency charities listing.

Will I get a tax receipt?

Yes. Official tax receipts are issued for eligible donations made through our donation form.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Choose the monthly option on the donation form. Monthly gifts let us plan teacher pay and subsidized seats for the whole year.

Can my business sponsor a program?

Yes. Fill in the sponsorship form on this page and we will send you our sponsorship package with recognition options.

Other ways to help

  • Volunteer your time on Sundays or at events
  • Book the Centre for your event. Rental income supports our programs
  • Share our programs with friends and family
