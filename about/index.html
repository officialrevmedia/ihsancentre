About us

About Al-Ihsan Learning Centre

A community learning centre in Ancaster, teaching traditional Islamic knowledge and serving local families.

Who we are

Al-Ihsan Learning Centre (Ihsan Centre) runs classes, gatherings and community support from our home at 487 Shaver Road in Ancaster. Our name comes from ihsan, excellence in worship and in how we treat people, and that is the standard we try to hold ourselves to.

On Sundays the building fills with children in Enlightened Hearts. During the week we host a preschool circle, adult programs, a book club, brothers' evenings and our monthly Mawlid. Throughout the year we run food drives, iftars, emergency aid and support for new Muslims and newcomer families.

We are a registered Canadian charity (763444353RR0001), run largely by volunteers and funded by our community.

What we care about

A place to gather

Potlucks, celebrations and evenings that bring families together.

Serving others

Food drives, Ramadan iftars, emergency aid and support for new Muslims.

Children and youth

A safe, joyful place to make friends and grow in faith and confidence.

Support the Centre

Every program here is kept open by donors.
