Rental questions

What kinds of events can I host?

Nikkahs, engagements, walimas and dinners, aqiqahs, iftars, birthdays and family celebrations, as well as community meetings, workshops and classes.

Can you help with decorations?

Yes. We work with decorators who can provide backdrops, flowers, stage and table styling. Tell us what you have in mind on the enquiry form and we will connect you.

How much does it cost?

We keep our rates affordable for the community. Pricing depends on the type of event, how long you need the space and the setup. Send an enquiry and we will reply with a quote.

How far ahead should I book?

As early as you can. Weekends and Ramadan evenings fill up first.

Are there any house rules?

The Centre is an Islamic space, so events follow Islamic etiquette, including no alcohol. We ask every guest to treat the space with care.
