A weekend education program for children and youth ages 4 to 16. An interactive and engaging space where young hearts are nurtured in the beauty of Islam through the study of Allah Almighty, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the Qur'an, noble character, Islamic arts, memorization, and more.

Age-Appropriate Learning

Five levels, rooted in tradition

Each level is named after a blessed tree mentioned in the Qur'an, reflecting the growth and nourishment we seek for every child at every stage of their journey.

🌿
Ages 4 to 5

The Fig Tree

Stories, songs, memorization, daily duas, and a growing love for Allah and His Messenger ﷺ.

🍇
Ages 6 to 9

The Grape Tree

Two levels (ages 6 to 7 and 8 to 9): Seerah, Shamaail, noble character, memorization, poetry, and Al-Ghazali readings.

🌺
Ages 9 to 11

The Pomegranate Tree

Aqidah, Shamaail, Adab, inquiry-based learning, memorization, and poetry.

🌴
Ages 12 to 16

The Date Palm Tree

Aqidah, Seerah, noble character, Fiqh, Halal and Haram, critical reasoning, and principled engagement.

The Olive Tree: Preschool Circle & Story Time

An engaging class for our youngest learners, ages 0 to 4, centred on stories and song. Who We Are

A beacon of hope in Ancaster.

At Ihsan Centre, we are committed to reviving traditional Islamic knowledge and fostering a spiritually uplifting environment through classes, gatherings, events, and community support initiatives. Located in the heart of Ancaster, Ihsan Centre is more than a space. It is a beacon of hope, a social gathering place, and a growing initiative rooted in sincerity, service, and the pursuit of excellence.

From weekly circles of Qur'an and dhikr to food drives, iftars, and emergency aid, Ihsan Centre is a community hub where families come together, children thrive, and hearts are nourished.

Faith & AqidahCommunity ServiceFellowship & UnityIhsan (Excellence)

Gathering & Social Space

A warm, welcoming environment where families come together for social events, potlucks, celebrations, and meaningful connections that strengthen the bonds of brotherhood and sisterhood.

Giving Back & Khidma

Through food drives, Ramadan iftars, emergency aid, support for new Muslims, and family referral services, Ihsan Centre serves the most vulnerable in our community with compassion.

A Place for Kids & Youth

Children and youth find a safe, joyful space to build friendships, explore their faith, develop confidence, and grow into caring, principled young people.

Get Involved

Volunteer with us.
