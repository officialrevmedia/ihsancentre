Who We Are

A beacon of hope in Ancaster.

At Ihsan Centre, we are committed to reviving traditional Islamic knowledge and fostering a spiritually uplifting environment through classes, gatherings, events, and community support initiatives. Located in the heart of Ancaster, Ihsan Centre is more than a space — it is a beacon of hope, a social gathering place, and a growing initiative rooted in sincerity, service, and the pursuit of excellence.

From weekly circles of Qur'an and dhikr to food drives, iftars, and emergency aid, Ihsan Centre is a community hub where families come together, children thrive, and hearts are nourished.

More Than a Masjid

A hub for the entire community.

Ihsan Centre is where families gather, children play and learn, and hearts are nourished through faith, friendship, and service to others.

Gathering & Social Space

A warm, welcoming environment where families come together for social events, potlucks, celebrations, and meaningful connections that strengthen the bonds of brotherhood and sisterhood.

Giving Back & Khidma

Through food drives, Ramadan iftars, emergency aid, support for new Muslims, and family referral services, Ihsan Centre is dedicated to serving the most vulnerable in our community with compassion.

A Place for Kids & Youth

Children and youth find a safe, joyful space to build friendships, explore their faith, develop confidence, and grow into caring, principled young people through age-appropriate programs and mentorship.

Class Groups

Age-appropriate learning journeys.

Each class is named after a blessed tree mentioned in the Qur'an, reflecting the growth and nourishment we seek for every child.

Ages 3–4

The Olive Tree

Ages 3–4 · Sr. Faiza

A gentle circle and story time program designed for little hearts. Teaching young children a deep connection to Allah and His Prophet through crafts, movement, songs, and storytelling.

Ages 5–6

The Fig Tree

Ages 5–6 · Sr. Danya

Rooted in foundational knowledge, this class nurtures a deep love for Allah and His Messenger through stories, songs, and memorization of short surahs from Juz 'Amma.

Ages 7–8

The Grape Tree

Ages 7–8 · Sr. Iffat

Living lessons that nourish heart and mind through key Seerah events, stories of the Prophet, and noble character cultivation using the Al-Ghazali Children's Series.

Ages 9–11

The Pomegranate Tree

Ages 9–11 · Sr. Ateeka & Br. Hassan

Building on intellectual interests through Aqidah, Sira Isma'il, Adab, inquiry-based units, and age-appropriate topics from the Al-Ghazali series.

Ages 12–16

The Date Palm Tree

Ages 12–16 · Br. Asim & Br. Yusuf

Critical reasoning anchored in tradition: Aqidah, Seerah, Adab, and discussions on contemporary issues that shape the spiritual and intellectual lives of our youth.

Ongoing & Upcoming

Our gatherings & programs.

From weekly circles to monthly celebrations, we offer a range of programs that nurture the heart, mind, and soul.

Circle of Light

Weekly · Qur'an & Dhikr

A weekly circle of Qur'an recitation and dhikr to nurture the heart and soul. Open to all members of the community seeking spiritual nourishment and connection.

In Praise of the Beloved

Monthly · Selected Sundays

A monthly Mawlid honouring the Prophet ﷺ with poetry, nasheeds, stories, and reflection. A gathering that brings hearts together in love.

Enlightened Hearts

Sundays · 10 AM to 1 PM

Our flagship weekend education program for children and youth ages 3 to 16. A full year of Qur'an, Aqidah, Adab, Seerah, Islamic Arts, character building, and mentorship.
